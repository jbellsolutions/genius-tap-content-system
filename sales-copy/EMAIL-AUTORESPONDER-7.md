- Non-converters enter a monthly "value newsletter" with ongoing extraction tips and case studies

- Test all 3 subject lines per email in equal rotation
- Winner determination: open rate for subject lines, click rate for body variations
- Minimum sample size: 200 sends per variation before declaring winner
