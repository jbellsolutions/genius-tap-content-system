# LinkedIn Post Template

## Specifications

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Character Limit | 3,000 (target 1,800-2,200) |
| Hook Fold | First 210 characters must stand alone (before "see more") |
| Paragraph Style | Single-line paragraphs with line breaks between |
| Max Paragraph Length | 3 lines |
| Hashtags | 3-5, placed at bottom |
| Emojis | Max 3 per post, never in hook line |
| CTA | Single, substantive (no "Agree?" or "Thoughts?") |
| Story Requirement | At least 1 story bank entry per post |

---

## Post Structure

```
[HOOK: 1-2 lines. Must stop the scroll. Lands before the "see more" fold.]

[SPACE]

[BODY SECTION 1: Setup or context. 1-3 short paragraphs.]

[BODY SECTION 2: Story or proof. The meat. 2-5 short paragraphs.]

[BODY SECTION 3: Lesson, reframe, or insight. 1-3 short paragraphs.]

[SPACE]

[CTA: 1-2 lines. Specific action or genuine conversation starter.]

[SPACE]

#Hashtag1 #Hashtag2 #Hashtag3 #Hashtag4
```

## Hook Patterns by DNA Style

### Hormozi: Contrarian Claim

Structure: A bold statement that contradicts what the audience assumes to be true. Often starts with "Stop" or "The worst" or a direct challenge.

```
Stop hiring for experience. It's the most expensive mistake in your industry.

I lost $87K learning this. Here's what nobody tells you about "rock star" hires:
```

```
Your pricing strategy is backwards.

Most [industry] professionals charge based on time. The top 2% charge based on something completely different.
```

```
I fired our best salesperson last March.

Revenue went up 34% the following quarter. Here's why.
```

### Mueller: Story Cold Open

Structure: Drop the reader directly into a scene. No setup, no context. Sensory details, action, tension.

```
The phone rang at 2:14 AM.

I knew before I picked up that something had gone sideways. The only question was how bad.
```

```
Three words into the pitch, I knew we were done.

The prospect leaned back, crossed her arms, and said the five-word sentence that changed how I sell forever.
```

```
"You're making a huge mistake."

That's what my mentor told me when I turned down the $400K contract. Everyone on my team agreed with him.
```

### Todd Brown: Mechanism Reveal

Structure: Identify a common problem, then tease that there is a hidden mechanism (cause) that nobody talks about.

```
There's a reason your [pipeline/funnel/team] keeps stalling at the same point.

And it's not what any LinkedIn guru is telling you.
```

```
I've identified 3 types of [problem]. Only one of them is actually about [obvious cause].

The other two? They're caused by something you probably think is working fine.
```

```
Everyone talks about [common solution] for [problem].

Nobody talks about the mechanism that makes [common solution] fail 73% of the time.
```

### Jay Abraham: Strategic Reframe

Structure: Elevate the conversation. Show that the audience is competing on the wrong axis entirely.

```
Most [role] compete on price. The top 1% compete on something else entirely.

It's not quality. It's not service. It's not even results. It's this:
```

```
You don't have a sales problem.

You have a positioning problem disguised as a sales problem. Here's the difference:
```

```
The difference between a $50K business and a $500K business isn't what you think.

It's not more clients. It's not more hours. It's not better marketing. It's one strategic decision.
```

### Buchan: Pattern Interrupt / Humor

Structure: Open with something unexpected, absurd, or self-deprecating that makes the reader double-take.

```
I accidentally sent a client our internal "worst-case scenario" document.

It became the best sales conversation we've ever had. Here's why being accidentally honest might be your best strategy:
```

```
My 7-year-old just gave me better business advice than my $15K mastermind.

She said: "Dad, if they don't like it, just make a different one." And she's right. Here's why:
```

```
I ran the numbers on my "productivity" last quarter.

Turns out I spent more time in meetings about meetings than actually doing work. The exact number was embarrassing.
```

### Bilyeu: Identity Challenge

Structure: Reframe the problem as an identity issue, not a tactics issue. Challenge who the reader is being, not what they are doing.

```
You don't have a [business problem] problem.

You have an identity problem. And until you fix who you're being, no tactic will save you.
```

```
The version of you that built this business can't be the version that scales it.

That's not a motivational quote. It's a structural reality. Here's what I mean:
```

```
Every day you're choosing between two versions of yourself.

One makes decisions based on fear. The other makes decisions based on where they want to be in 3 years. Most people don't realize they're choosing.
```

### Brian Kurtz: Insider Authority

Structure: Invoke earned authority, name-drop mentors/legends naturally (not manufactured), offer insider knowledge.

```
I learned this from [respected figure] over dinner at [specific place].

He said one sentence that changed how I think about [topic]. I've never shared it publicly until now.
```

```
There's a reason the most successful [role] in [industry] all do this one thing.

I noticed the pattern after interviewing 40+ of them. None of them mentioned it first. I had to dig.
```

```
Twenty years in this industry and I'm still surprised by what actually moves the needle.

Last month I discovered something that contradicts almost everything I've taught. Here it is:
```

### Lead Gen Jay: Tactical Specificity

Structure: Promise a specific, concrete takeaway. Use numbers and specificity in the hook.

```
Here's the exact 4-step process that generated 127 qualified leads last month.

Total ad spend: $0. Total time: 2 hours per week. Steal this:
```

```
I just audited 23 [industry] [pipelines/funnels/processes].

Every single one had the same gap. Here's what it is and the 3-minute fix:
```

```
Most people over-complicate [process].

Here's the entire system on one page. No fluff. Just the steps that actually produce [outcome]:
```

### Liam Ottley: Technical Demystification

Structure: Take a hyped or misunderstood technology/trend and explain what is actually happening.

```
Everyone's talking about [trend/technology].

Almost nobody understands what it actually does. Here's the 2-minute explanation that will put you ahead of 95% of your industry:
```

```
I built [specific thing] last week using [technology].

Here's what worked, what broke, and what I'd do differently. No hype. Just what actually happened:
```

```
The [technology] hype cycle has reached peak confusion.

Here's what's actually real, what's marketing, and what you should actually pay attention to right now:
```

## CTA Patterns

### Conversation Starters (High Engagement)
- "What's your version of this story? I'm genuinely curious."
- "If you've been through something similar, I want to hear about it."
- "What's the most expensive lesson you've learned in [specific area]?"
- "Tell me: which of these resonates with your experience?"

### Value Offers (Lead Generation)
- "I put the full breakdown in a doc. Comment [WORD] and I'll send it to you."
- "Want the template? DM me '[WORD]' and it's yours."
- "I'm writing a deeper guide on this. Drop your best [topic] tip below and I'll make sure you get it first."

### Direct CTAs (Authority Building)
- "If this changed how you think about [topic], share it with someone who needs to hear it."
- "Follow for more breakdowns like this. I post [frequency]."
- "I help [specific audience] do [specific outcome]. If that's you, the link in my profile is a good next step."

### Avoid These CTAs
- "Agree?" (lazy)
- "Thoughts?" (lazy)
- "Drop a [emoji] if you relate" (engagement bait)
- "Like if you..." (engagement bait)
- "Comment below!" (generic)
- "Share this with your network" (presumptuous without earning it)

## Hashtag Strategy

**Format**: 3-5 hashtags, last line of the post, separated by spaces.

**Mix**:
- 1-2 broad hashtags (high volume): #Leadership #Sales #Marketing #Entrepreneurship #B2B
- 1-2 niche hashtags (targeted): #AgencyGrowth #B2BSaaS #StaffingIndustry #HomeServices
- 1 branded or topic-specific hashtag: #HiringMistakes #PricingStrategy #ContentMarketing

**Rules**:
- No more than 5 hashtags total
- Do not use hashtags in the body of the post
- Do not start a post with a hashtag
- Rotate hashtags week to week to avoid looking automated
- Research hashtag following counts -- avoid hashtags with under 1K followers

## LinkedIn Algorithm Notes

These are not rules but operational awareness for the Creator:

- Posts with "see more" clicks perform better than short posts (front-load a compelling hook)
- Dwell time matters. Longer posts that hold attention outperform short posts that get scrolled past.
- Comments from the author in the first hour boost reach. The Creator should suggest a "first comment" for the client to post.
- PDF/document posts get very high reach. Consider occasional carousel-style posts using document uploads.
- Posting time matters less than consistency, but 7-9 AM and 5-7 PM local time tend to perform well.
- LinkedIn suppresses posts with external links. If linking out, put the link in the first comment, not the post body.

## First Comment Suggestion

Every LinkedIn post should include a suggested first comment for the client to post immediately after publishing. This boosts the algorithm and adds value.

```
Suggested first comment:
"[Additional context, a question, a resource link, or a P.S. that extends the post's value without repeating it]"
```

Examples:
- "P.S. - If you want the full breakdown of this framework, I put it in a free doc. Link in my profile."
- "I should add: the person I almost didn't hire? They just closed our biggest deal ever. Sometimes the best decisions feel wrong at the time."
- "Curious: for those of you who've made this mistake too -- how long did it take you to realize it?"
