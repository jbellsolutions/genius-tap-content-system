Your Journey to Infinite Content

Each step is designed to extract, refine, and multiply your expertise into authentic content that sounds unmistakably like you.

Step 01

The Intake

15 minutes

Client fills a structured form covering career moments, areas of expertise, content goals, and audience insights. This primes the extraction engine before we ever speak.

Step 02

The Extraction Call

60 – 90 minutes

A guided interview using conversational triggers. We run the "Chapters" exercise, the "Who was there?" drill-down, and uncover signature stories only you can tell.

Step 03

The Live Edit

30 – 45 minutes

Direct-response copywriting applied to your existing content in real time. We show before/after transformations so you see the power of the system instantly.

Step 04

The Voice Build

2 – 3 hours (internal)

We process all artifacts, match your voice to 2–3 copywriter DNA styles, and configure the multi-agent pipeline that will produce your content at scale.

Step 05

The Calibration

1 – 2 days

You review 5 content samples and answer one question: "Does this sound like me?" Your feedback tunes the system until every word feels authentically yours.

Step 06

The Launch

Week 1

Content production goes live. You receive 2 posts per day, 3 articles per week, and 2 newsletters per week — all in your voice, ready to publish.

Step 07

The Optimization

Monthly

Performance review, new story extraction, voice refinement. The system gets sharper every month as we feed it fresh material and engagement data.
