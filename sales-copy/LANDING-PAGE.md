### The AI Slop Epidemic

Your LinkedIn feed is drowning in content that sounds like it was written by a robot with a thesaurus. "In today's fast-paced business landscape..." You've seen it. You've cringed at it. And you've probably noticed that some of the smartest people you know are publishing it with their names attached — because they don't know what else to do.

### The Expert's Paradox

You have 10, 20, 30 years of hard-won expertise. Real stories. Real scars. Real lessons that could change how people think about your industry. But when you sit down to write a post, your brain short-circuits. Too many stories. Too much nuance. Too little time. So you post nothing — and the person who read one book last month owns the conversation.

### The Ghostwriter Trap

You tried hiring a ghostwriter. They interviewed you for 30 minutes, asked surface-level questions, and produced content that sounded vaguely like your industry but nothing like YOU. It was polished, professional, and completely generic. You could swap in any other expert's name and nobody would notice the difference.

## FAQ

**Do I need to prepare anything before the call?**
No. Show up and talk. The extraction method is designed to surface stories you didn't know you were going to tell. Preparation actually works against the process — we want the unfiltered version, not the rehearsed one.

**How is this different from hiring a ghostwriter?**
Ghostwriters write content inspired by you. We extract content that IS you. The difference is specificity: our process pulls out stories with named characters, specific details, and real dialogue that no ghostwriter would think to ask about. Every piece passes our 3-Agent Quality Gauntlet to ensure it couldn't have been written by anyone else.

**You use AI — so how is this not AI slop?**
Our AI agents are trained on frameworks from 9 master copywriters and constrained by your specific stories and voice. The AI doesn't generate ideas — it structures YOUR stories in YOUR voice. Then three separate quality agents check every piece against our anti-slop criteria. The result sounds like you on your best day, not like ChatGPT on its default setting.

**What if I'm not a good storyteller?**
You don't need to be. You need to have lived an interesting career — and if you've been in your field for more than a decade, you have. Our guided questions do the heavy lifting. The stories are already in your head; we just ask the questions nobody else thinks to ask.

**How long until I see content?**
Your first batch of content is delivered within 10 business days of the extraction call. Monthly clients receive ongoing content on a rolling schedule.

**What if I want to cancel the monthly service?**
Cancel anytime. No contracts. No penalties. Your story bank is yours to keep regardless. We stay because the content works, not because of fine print.
