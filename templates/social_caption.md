# Social Caption Template

## Specifications

| Platform | Character Limit | Hashtag Rules | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Twitter/X | 280 characters | No hashtags in body (optional 1-2 at end) | Punchy, conversational, one idea |
| Instagram Feed | 2,200 characters (aim 125-200 for feed, up to 500 for carousel) | 5-10 in first comment, not caption | Visual-first, story snippets, personal |
| TikTok | 150 characters | 2-3 in caption | Casual, hook-first, trend-aware |

---

## Universal Social Caption Structure

```
[HOOK: First line. Must stop the scroll. This is the only line most people read.]

[CORE: 1-3 sentences. The single idea, insight, or punchline.]

[CTA: Optional. Keep it tight. A question, a tag prompt, or nothing.]
```

Every caption is one idea. Not two. Not "three quick tips." One idea, delivered with enough force to stop someone mid-scroll.

## Twitter/X Templates

### The One-Liner (Hormozi DNA)

Single sentence that reframes a common belief. No setup, no explanation. The insight stands alone.

```
Your "dream client" isn't someone who pays the most.
It's someone who gets the best results and tells everyone about it.
```

```
The difference between $10K months and $50K months
isn't more clients. It's fewer clients at higher prices.
```

```
If your sales process requires "overcoming objections,"
your positioning is broken.
```

### The Mini-Story (Mueller DNA)

A story compressed to its absolute essence. One scene, one turn, one lesson.

```
Turned down a $400K contract last year.
My team thought I was insane.

That decision led to a $1.2M partnership 6 months later.

Sometimes the best move is the one that terrifies your spreadsheet.
```

```
My first hire cost me $87K in damage.
My second hire is now my VP of Sales.

The only difference: I stopped hiring resumes
and started hiring character.
```

### The Contrarian Take (Hormozi DNA)

Challenge something the audience believes. One sentence setup, one sentence punch.

```
Unpopular opinion: "consistency" is overrated.

What matters is consistently doing the RIGHT things.
Consistently doing the wrong things just makes you predictably mediocre.
```

```
Hot take: Your content doesn't need to "go viral."
It needs to make 50 of the right people think, "This person gets it."
```

### The Tactical Nugget (Lead Gen Jay DNA)

One specific, actionable insight. No fluff.

```
The fastest way to improve your close rate:
Stop presenting solutions before the prospect describes
their problem in their own words.

That's it. That's the tactic.
```

```
Want more replies on cold outreach?
Lead with something specific about THEM.
Not "I help companies like yours" but "I saw your Q3 numbers and noticed [X]."
```

### The Question (Jay Abraham DNA)

A question that makes the reader stop and actually think.

```
What would your business look like if you only
kept the clients who were genuinely happy?

Most people are afraid to answer that honestly.
```

```
If you couldn't use your current service/product anymore,
would your clients find someone else immediately?

If yes: you're a commodity.
If no: you've built something real.
```

### The Technical Insight (Liam Ottley DNA)

Demystify something complex in one tweet.

```
"AI will replace you" is wrong.

A person who uses AI well will replace a person who doesn't.
The tool isn't the threat. The skill gap is.
```

```
Everyone wants to "build an AI agent."
Most people don't need one.

You need an automated workflow.
The distinction matters because it's 10x cheaper and actually works.
```

## Instagram Caption Templates

### Feed Post Caption (Short: 125-200 characters)

Used for single-image or reel posts. The caption supports the visual.

```
[HOOK - one sentence that gives context to the visual]

[CORE - the insight, tied to what's shown]

[TAG CTA - optional: "Tag someone who needs this" or a question]
```

Example shapes:

```
This is what my calendar looked like the month
everything changed.

Not more meetings. Fewer meetings.
Better meetings. With better people.

What does your ideal week look like?
```

```
3 years between these two photos.
Same office. Same industry. Completely different business.

The only thing I changed was who I said "no" to.
```

### Carousel Caption (Longer: 200-500 characters)

Used for slide-based educational content. The caption frames the carousel.

```
[HOOK - why the reader should swipe through]

[CONTEXT - brief setup for what the carousel covers]

[SWIPE CTA - "Swipe to see all [X]" or "Save this for later"]
```

Example shape:

```
I lost $87K on one bad hire.

Then I built a 4-question interview framework that
has produced 3 A-players in a row.

Swipe through for the exact questions and what to
listen for in the answers.

Save this. You'll need it next time you're hiring.
```

### Instagram-Specific Rules
- Hashtags go in the FIRST COMMENT, not the caption
- 5-10 hashtags per post (do not overdo it)
- Use a mix of size tiers: 2-3 large (500K+ posts), 3-4 medium (50K-500K), 2-3 small/niche (under 50K)
- Line breaks matter on Instagram -- use them for readability
- Emojis are more acceptable on Instagram than LinkedIn. Match client voice.
- Tag relevant accounts when genuine (not spam-tagging for reach)

## TikTok Caption Templates

### The Hook Caption (150 characters max)

TikTok captions support the video hook. They are not standalone content. The caption should complement what is being said in the video.

```
[VIDEO HOOK REINFORCE - supports the first thing said in the video]
#Hashtag1 #Hashtag2
```

Example shapes:

```
The $87K hiring mistake that made me change everything
#businesstips #hiring
```

```
Stop doing this in your sales calls (seriously)
#sales #b2b
```

```
POV: you finally figure out why your pipeline stalls at the same point
#business #salesprocess
```

### TikTok-Specific Rules
- Under 150 characters
- 2-3 hashtags, integrated or at end
- Match the tone of the video (casual, direct)
- Do not write formal captions for TikTok
- Use trending audio references in caption when relevant
- Captions should make someone want to watch, not replace the video content

## Cross-Platform Adaptation Rules

When the same idea is being posted across platforms, it must be adapted, not copy-pasted.

### The Same Idea on 3 Platforms

**Core idea**: "I lost $87K on a bad hire. The fix was a 4-question interview framework."

**Twitter/X version** (punchy, one-idea):
```
My worst hire cost me $87K.
My best hire came from asking 4 questions
that have nothing to do with the resume.

The difference wasn't the candidates. It was the filter.
```

**LinkedIn version** (story-driven, longer, with hook):
```
I hired a "rock star" last March.

Perfect resume. Crushed the interview. Everyone on the team was excited.

Three months later I was writing a check for $87K in damages
and apologizing to the two best people who quit because of that hire.

[FULL STORY + FRAMEWORK + CTA]
```

**Instagram version** (visual-first, caption supports):
```
3 months. $87K. 2 good employees gone.
All because I hired a resume instead of a person.

Swipe for the 4-question framework that fixed my hiring.
Save this before your next interview.
```

### Adaptation Rules
- Never post the same text on multiple platforms
- Lead with the platform's native strength (Twitter = wit, LinkedIn = story, Instagram = visual, TikTok = hook)
- Same core idea, different execution
- Stagger posting across platforms (do not post the same idea on all three on the same day)
- Twitter is the shortest take, LinkedIn is the fullest, Instagram is the most visual

## Social Caption Anti-Slop Rules

In addition to global anti-slop rules:

- No "Thread" emoji followed by numbered list (unless genuinely structured as a thread)
- No "1/ [point]" threading format on Instagram or TikTok
- No "HUGE announcement" unless it is genuinely huge
- No "I don't usually do this but..." (yes you do, it's a content formula)
- No engagement bait: "Like if you agree" / "RT if you relate" / "Save this"
- No fake vulnerability: "I was terrified to share this" on a mild take
- No "this is going to be controversial" on an opinion everyone shares
- No screenshot of your own tweet posted to Instagram
- If it starts with "Reminder:" it better be reminding people of something genuinely useful, not a platitude
