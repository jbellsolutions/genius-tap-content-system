### Day 2 (Tuesday): The Strait of Hormuz Story

**Content type:** Standalone LinkedIn post

**Source story:** Story #1 (The Strait of Hormuz at Midnight)

**Hook:** "The last time the USS John F. Kennedy went through the Strait of Hormuz, I had the deck. It was midnight."

**Hook style:** Bill Mueller (story-driven, scene-setting, named characters, the punchline arrives at the end)

**Body:** Set the scene -- Iranian radio, Omani radio, cruiser out ahead, oiler in trail, things he can't mention underneath them. Ships coming and going. Then the Captain walks onto the bridge. The call sign. The cigar line.

**Close:** "That was the night I learned what trust looks like. Not a certificate. Not a title. A commanding officer walking away because he knew you had it." Connects to the theme of leadership and earned trust.

**Engagement potential:** VERY HIGH. Military content performs extremely well on LinkedIn, especially stories with cinematic detail. The Strait of Hormuz is in the news cycle. Veterans will share this aggressively. The "cigar" punchline is memorable and quotable.

**Tagging strategy:**
- Tag any Navy connections Tony has on LinkedIn
- Tag veteran organizations or groups
- Anyone connected to the JFK carrier community

**Hashtags:** #Navy #Leadership #Trust #MilitaryService #StraitOfHormuz #VeteranStories
