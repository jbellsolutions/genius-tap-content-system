## POST 5: The Expert's Paradox
**Style: Jay Abraham (Value/Insight)**

The best experts create the worst content.

When you've spent 20+ years mastering something, your knowledge becomes compressed. What took you a decade to learn, you now explain in a sentence. What was a pivotal career moment becomes "oh, that was just a Tuesday."
